Our Story

Rooted in Ethiopian
Coffee Heritage

Abinet Admasu Coffee, operating as RAY General Trading, is an Ethiopian coffee exporting company built on deep roots in the Sidama region — one of the world's most celebrated coffee origins.

Founded with a clear vision of connecting Ethiopia's extraordinary coffee heritage with international markets, we combine traditional Ethiopian coffee culture with modern export standards. Our verified trade activity includes successful large-volume shipments to North American markets — including Canada and the United States — establishing us as a credible and experienced global supplier.

We work directly with farming communities across Sidama's finest zones — Bensa, Bona, and Burra — ensuring full traceability, quality consistency, and long-term sustainability in every lot we export.

Traceability & Sustainability

Transparent from
Farm to Port

Modern international buyers demand full chain-of-custody transparency. Our traceability system documents every step from farm-level sourcing to export container.

We maintain detailed lot records, farmer partnership data, GPS farm coordinates, processing station logs, and cupping records for every shipment — meeting the highest standards required by specialty importers and compliant with emerging EUDR regulations.
